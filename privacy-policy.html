We may use Google Ads and remarketing technologies to:

6. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • • Analytics

  • • Understanding website usage

Non-essential analytics and advertising cookies may be subject to consent requirements depending on applicable law and the user's location.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal and business information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including:

  • • Providing or completing requested services
  • • Maintaining project and business records
  • • Handling legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations
  • • Resolving disputes
  • • Maintaining security and preventing misuse

Certain information may need to be retained for longer periods where required by applicable law. Where appropriate and legally permitted, you may request deletion of personal information that is no longer required for a legitimate or legal purpose.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to:

  • • Request information about personal data we process about you
  • • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • • Request deletion of personal information where legally applicable
  • • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • • Raise a grievance or privacy-related complaint
  • • Request information about how your personal data is being processed

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

To exercise a privacy right or raise a privacy concern, please contact us using the details below.

If you have provided consent for a specific processing activity and wish to withdraw it, you can contact us using the contact details provided in this Privacy Policy.
