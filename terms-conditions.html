SECTION 02

2. Scope of Engineering & Consulting Services

Websirg provides elite digital technology services including, but not limited to:

  • • Mobile Application Engineering: Native iOS (Swift), Android (Kotlin), and cross-platform (Flutter/React Native) architectures.
  • • Custom Web Platforms & CRM: Bespoke enterprise panels, customer relationship backbones, RESTful/GraphQL APIs, and cloud microservices.

  • • Digital Product Design: UI/UX wireframes, interactive prototypes, and production-ready Figma design systems.

All engagements are governed by mutually agreed project specifications, statements of work, or sprint backlogs defining deliverables and milestones.

SECTION 03

3. Agile Sprints, Testing & Acceptance Milestones

We work on structured two-week agile sprint cycles. For milestone-based deliveries:

  • • Upon completion of each milestone, Websirg deploys the build to a dedicated staging environment for Client Quality Assurance (QA).
  • • The Client is granted five (5) business days to review deliverables against agreed acceptance criteria and provide consolidated revision requests.
  • • In the absence of written feedback or rejection within the review window, deliverables are deemed accepted, allowing subsequent sprint kickoff.

4. Intellectual Property Rights & Code Ownership

100% Client Ownership Guarantee: Upon full and final settlement of all milestone invoices, all custom code, graphic assets, database schemas, and intellectual property developed exclusively for the Client transfer entirely to the Client.

Websirg retains ownership of pre-existing proprietary developer toolkits, common code libraries, boilerplate frameworks, and third-party open-source components, which are licensed to the Client on a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free basis to run their system.

  • • Websirg enforces strict non-disclosure obligations across all developers, contractors, and project managers.

  • • Client proprietary data will never be repurposed, sold, or shared with third parties without express written authorization.

Invoicing schedules are structured based on the approved Statement of Work:

  • • All invoices are payable within seven (7) business days of receipt unless explicitly modified in writing. Applicable statutory taxes (GST/TDS) are charged as mandated by Indian law.
